Letter grades and course credit hours are not present in the verified source files and are therefore not invented.

SGPA and CGPA formulas

SGPA = Σ(course grade point × course credit hours) ÷ Σ(semester credit hours)

CGPA = Σ(all quality points) ÷ Σ(all attempted credit hours)

The portal displays the official supplied SGPA/CGPA values because source credit hours are unavailable.

Illustrative calculation

If two example courses both carry 3 credits with grade points 4.0 and 3.0, SGPA = (4×3 + 3×3) ÷ 6 = 3.50. This is a formula illustration, not a student record.

Common questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I check a result?

Select Check Result, enter the complete roll number, and choose Search result. Letter case and surrounding spaces are ignored.

How do I download a transcript or result card?

Open the student record and use its print or PDF action. In the browser print dialog, select Save as PDF for a digital copy.

Why is a semester or value unavailable?

The portal shows only verified source data. Missing information is not estimated or invented.

Does MZ affect CGPA?

No. MZ (Zero Math) is a zero-credit-hour subject and does not affect SGPA or CGPA.

User guide

Help

Roll-number format

Use the complete university roll number, such as ECTE231122145. Letter case and spaces are normalized.

Browser and mobile support

Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari browser. Wide tables can be swiped horizontally on phones.

Troubleshooting

If offline data appears outdated, reconnect once and reload so the current service-worker cache can update.

Contact support

Email arifranaarif66@gmail.com and include the roll number and a description, but do not send passwords.
